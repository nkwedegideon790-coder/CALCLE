About Us

Built by students who are flying the same route

CALCLE was started by CLE and CAL, two aviation students who kept getting asked the same questions by younger students and family friends — and decided the answers deserved a proper home.

We're currently working through aviation training ourselves, which means we know the forms, the deadlines, the entrance exams and the waiting that the official brochures never mention. CALCLE exists to pass that knowledge forward before it's learned the hard way.

We don't sell admission. We sit with you, look honestly at your results, budget and goals, and help you choose a realistic route into the industry — then stay with you through the paperwork that follows.

Our mission

To help aspiring aviation professionals make informed decisions about their training and career path, with guidance that's specific to their situation rather than generic advice copied from a forum.

Courses & Training

Six ways into the industry

Aviation is bigger than the flight deck. Here's a plain-language look at the main pathways we advise on.

01

Pilot training

Private and commercial licensing, flight hours, medical certification and choosing between local and overseas flight schools.

02

Flight dispatch

Flight planning, weather assessment and regulatory work that keeps every flight legally and safely on schedule.

03

Cabin crew

Passenger safety, service standards and the interview process airlines use to select cabin crew.

04

Aircraft maintenance

Aircraft Maintenance Engineering licensing (AME) and the technical training that keeps fleets airworthy.

05

Aviation management

Airport operations, airline administration and the business side of keeping aviation running.

06

Other aviation programmes

Air traffic control, safety and security, and specialist short courses — we'll help you find where you fit.

Do I need to already know what role I want?

No. Most people who reach out are choosing between two or three options — that comparison is exactly what a consultation is for.

Is CALCLE affiliated with NCAT or any airline?

No. We're an independent consultancy run by aviation students. We guide you through official processes; we don't represent any institution.

How soon before an application deadline should I reach out?

As early as possible — ideally a few months out, since document gathering and exam prep both take time.

Do you help with overseas flight schools too?

Yes, alongside Nigerian institutions like NCAT — we'll walk through what fits your budget and goals.
